Fin-CLARIAH Roadshow

University of Oulu, Wednesday, 26 August 2026
Tellus Stage

The Fin-CLARIAH Roadshow at the University of Oulu will bring together researchers and infrastructure specialists working on metadata, language resources, and research infrastructures for the humanities and social sciences. The event focuses particularly on metadata practices and interoperability in an era increasingly shaped by large language models, machine-readable corpora, and AI-assisted research workflows. Talks and roundtable discussions will address researcher profiles, CLARIN metadata standards, TurkuNLP tools, and broader metadata desiderata across the Finnish research infrastructure landscape.
